# Project Summary
> This project researches how potholes and road damage is detected in dashcams and how it can be used to help civil engineers and self-driving cars.

This project investigates the use of artificial intelligence for the automatic detection of potholes and road surface damage from dashcam images. Road inspections are traditionally carried out through manual surveys, which can be costly, time-consuming, and infrequent. Delayed identification of road defects can increase maintenance costs, cause vehicle damage, and create safety hazards for road users.

The proposed research explores image classification techniques to distinguish between damaged and undamaged road surfaces using a labelled image dataset. The study aims to evaluate the effectiveness of machine learning models in supporting civil engineering maintenance operations and improving road monitoring processes. The findings may contribute to more efficient infrastructure management and demonstrate the potential of AI-based road monitoring systems within civil engineering.
# Introduction
## Domain & Community
> The detection of potholes and road damages is important to help with civil engineering and self-driving cars.

This project is situated within the domain of Civil Engineering, specifically transportation infrastructure maintenance. The primary community includes civil engineers, municipal road authorities, transportation agencies, and infrastructure maintenance teams responsible for monitoring road conditions.

Road surface deterioration, including potholes and cracks, poses risks to public safety, vehicle integrity, and transport efficiency. Traditional inspection methods often require manual surveys, which are labor-intensive and may not cover large road networks effectively. Automated road damage detection using artificial intelligence has the potential to assist engineers in identifying defects more efficiently and prioritizing maintenance activities.
## AI Techniques & Tools
This project will use image classification techniques from the field of computer vision to identify whether dashcam images contain potholes or road surface damage. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) will be explored as the primary machine learning approach due to their effectiveness in image recognition tasks.

Development and experimentation will be conducted using Python and Google Colab. Libraries such as TensorFlow, Keras, NumPy, Pandas, and Matplotlib will be used for data preprocessing, model training, evaluation, and visualization.

# Aim & Objectives
## Aim
To develop and evaluate an AI-based image classification model capable of detecting potholes and road damage from dashcam images to support road maintenance and infrastructure monitoring.
## Objectives
1. Collect and prepare a labelled road damage dataset.
2. Train a CNN-based classification model.
3. Evaluate performance using accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score.
4. Compare findings against existing literature.
5. Assess practical applications for road maintenance monitoring.
